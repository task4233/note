# Privacy Policy

## Google Analytics
This site uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google LLC, to understand how the site is used (e.g. which pages are viewed and from which language version).

- Google Analytics uses cookies to collect data such as the pages you view, your approximate location, your browser and device. The data does not identify you by name.
- The collected data is processed by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) for details.
- You can disable cookies in your browser settings, or opt out with the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
